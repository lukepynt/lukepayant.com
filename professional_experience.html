UTFET - Wayfinder 1

To maximize the available workspace at my desk, I designed and 3D-printed a power strip mount. I then prepared a design report to practice explaining my reasoning behind important design decisions, demonstrate my competency in CAD and FDM 3D printing, and document the performance of this design after one year of daily use. The report is designed to provide a brief overview of the project on the first page, with additional detail available in subsequent pages.

This project was completed in August 2025. In May 2026, Section V of the report was updated to discuss the time-dependent deformation observed after one year of use.
